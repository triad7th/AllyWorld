AllyMetronomeLite Privacy Policy

Effective September 8, 2026

This policy describes the privacy practices of AllyMetronomeLite - Tap Tempo, published by AllyWorld.

Data collection

AllyMetronomeLite does not collect or transmit personal data to AllyWorld or to third-party analytics or advertising services. The app does not require an account, contain analytics or advertising SDKs, or track you across other apps and websites.

Information stored on your device

The app stores metronome preferences, such as tempo, meter, subdivision, sound and control visibility, locally on your device. It also stores local app-use counters and dates to decide when to display Apple's app-rating prompt. These values are not sent to AllyWorld.

Device motion is used locally for visual effects. The app synthesizes its metronome sounds and does not record microphone audio.

Apple services and external links

The optional full-app link opens the separate AllyMetronome listing on Apple's App Store. Apple handles App Store purchases, ratings and any diagnostics you choose to share under Apple's own privacy policies and your device settings. AllyMetronomeLite does not receive payment-card information.

Support

If you email us, we receive the email address and information you choose to include and use them to respond to your request. Please do not include sensitive personal information in support messages.

Changes and contact

We will update this page and its effective date if the app's privacy practices change. For privacy questions, contact allyworldchannel@gmail.com.
