Need help with AllyMetronomeLite - Tap Tempo? Email allyworldchannel@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version and a short description of the problem.

Getting started

Hold your iPhone in landscape. Set a tempo from 30 to 252 BPM with the arrow buttons, or use TAP to tap your tempo. Press PLAY to start and STOP to stop.

Meters and subdivisions

METER cycles through 0, 1, 2, 3 and 4. Meter 0 provides an unmetered click. PRESET cycles through 4/4, 3/4 and 2/4. SUBDIV changes the click subdivision, including eighth notes, triplets and sixteenth notes.

Sounds and controls

Open SETTINGS to choose Standard, Loud or Woodblock sounds and show or hide Tap, Preset and Meter controls. If you cannot hear the click, check your device volume and audio output.

Lite and the full app

Lite is a fully working metronome with a fixed /4 beat value and meters up to 4. The separate full AllyMetronome app adds meters up to 9, /8 beat values and additional presets. The Unlock all features button explains these options and links to the full app's App Store page.
