AllyScores privacy policy

Effective September 20, 2026

This policy describes how AllyScores, published by AllyWorld, handles information in its current app or preview.

Library and cached content

AllyScores uses browser storage for its library and locally cached score information. Clearing browser site data can remove that local information.

Google Drive and shared links

You can connect Google Drive to access your own files. Google authorization, an app token-exchange service, and tokens stored in your browser support that connection. Shared scores and books are fetched from the source indicated by their links; availability depends on the owner’s sharing permissions.

Your controls

Disconnect from the app account menu or revoke the connection in your Google Account. You can clear local browser data. The source owner controls sharing access to files and books.

Support messages

If you contact us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to respond to your request and understand the issue. Contact us if you want us to remove your support correspondence, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

Platforms and external services

Google processes information used by the optional Drive connection under Google’s privacy policy. You can manage connections in your Google Account.

When you visit this website or a web app, its hosting provider processes the requests needed to deliver the page, which can include an IP address, requested address, and browser information. This is separate from locally stored app records. The AllyWorld promotional site does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies.

Changes and contact

We update this page and its effective date when these practices change. For questions about privacy, email allyworldchannel@gmail.com.
